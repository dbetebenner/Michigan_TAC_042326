OEAA psychometrician. Present April 23, 2026, and presenter of
[[2026-04-subtest-performance-indicators]] on April 24 — the full morning of day two.

Author of [[ct-po-vs-sbac-simulation-deck]], the most technically substantial artifact in the
corpus: a comparison of a Classification Tree with Proportional Odds against the Smarter Balanced
subscore indicator method, with K-modes as reference, across two batches of simulated conditions
plus real-data explorations at grades 5, 8, and 11.

The work criticises the existing subscore literature on two grounds — that it treats subscore
reporting as prediction of true subtest performance, and that it assumes multivariate normality
(citing Sinharay 2014 and Gorney & Sinharay 2025) — and demonstrates empirically that Michigan's
science subtest distributions are not normal.

The committee described the work as "very sophisticated" and offered support:
[[rec-subscore-support-zeng-collaboration]].
