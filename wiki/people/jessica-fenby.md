Author of [[tac-meeting-notes-april-2026]], the official summary minutes — the single richest

The minutes' structure, particularly the consistent `TAC Question:` / `TAC Feedback:` markers, is

catalogued on that page and are the evidence base for
`documents/ai-native-tac.qmd`. The limitations are properties of the format and the timeline,
not of the drafting.
