The sole Office of Educator Excellence attendee at [[2026-04-23-tac-meeting]], present
April 23, 2026.

Lead presenter of [[2026-04-mttc-meaningfully-different-forms]] — the meeting's longest
session — alongside Nancy Hahn, Heather Klesch, and Jackie Thompson of [[pearson]].

The session sought committee guidance on two questions: whether OEE's working definition of
"meaningfully different" test forms was defensible
([[mttc-meaningfully-different-forms]]), and whether to build constructed-response tests for
optional science and social studies endorsements
([[mttc-constructed-response-endorsements]]). The committee rejected the first and advised
against the second.
