Member of the Michigan OEAA/OEE Technical Advisory Committee. Present both days of
[[2026-04-23-tac-meeting]].

Named twice in the April record — the only TAC member named in the body of the minutes at all.

**On the origin of Michigan's growth targets.** Asked how the AGP targets were initially set, the
answer given in [[2026-04-accountability-growth-targets]] was that "they were set out of the
R-set that Damian Betebenner built" — the R SGP package, which Michigan uses for quantile
regression growth targets on M-STEP, PSAT 8/9/10, SAT with Essay, and WIDA ACCESS. See
[[michigan-growth-model]].

**On subscore methodology.** In [[2026-04-subtest-performance-indicators]] he offered to
collaborate with [[ji-zeng]] on her subscore work —
[[rec-subscore-support-zeng-collaboration]]. The minutes render this as "Damion Betebenner"; the
name is corrected in this corpus.

**Note on this corpus.** This repo was built by Betebenner as a demonstration for the
September 10, 2026 TAC meeting, so he is both a subject of the record and its compiler. The
argument the corpus makes is set out in `documents/ai-native-tac.qmd`.
