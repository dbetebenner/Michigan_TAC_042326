**Raised by:** OEAA itself, in [[2026-04-accountability-growth-targets]].

**The situation.** The content-area model application scatterplot showed a meaningful impact on
overall rating, and the English Learner application showed the same magnitude of impact on the EL
Index. OEAA "has not determined if this is a desirable impact and would need to take some time
to research this."

**Why it is the hard question.** The [[nearest-grade-average-method]] was proposed to fix
over-identification of high schools. Whether it *fixes* the problem or merely *moves* it depends
on where the identifications land after the change, and on whether the schools newly identified
are ones anyone believes should be. A method that produces more reasonable targets and less
defensible identifications is not obviously an improvement.

Note that this question interacts with [[rec-growth-adequate-target-above-65]]: raising the
adequacy threshold at the same time as adopting NGA would change the impact again, in the
opposite direction.
