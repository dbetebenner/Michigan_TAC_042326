**Asked by:** the committee, in [[2026-04-accountability-growth-targets]].

**Answer given:** OEAA "hasn't gotten that far in planning and is still questioning whether or
not a new growth calculation should be done and how we would communicate this to schools."

**Why it matters.** Adopting the [[nearest-grade-average-method]] touches all grades and would
require an announced break in trend — every longitudinal comparison across the boundary year
becomes invalid. An announced break has to be announced *before* the results, not explained
after them, and schools under accountability pressure will read any discontinuity in their
favour or against it depending on which way it moves.

This is the practical face of [[essa-plan-constraints]] and it is currently unplanned.
