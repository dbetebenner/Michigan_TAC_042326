**Asked by:** [[pearson]], in [[2026-04-mttc-meaningfully-different-forms]].

**Answer given:** "Yes, but it is dependent upon so many variables. Need to define the total time
of reaching testing counts vs. rate of testing in next contract."

**Why it is still open.** The answer names the trade without resolving it, and explicitly defers
it to the next contract. It is the other half of
[[q-mttc-examinee-accumulation-rate]]: accumulating 100 examinees slowly enough that the earliest
of them no longer represent the current candidate population defeats the purpose of waiting for
them.

For low-incidence fields this may mean there is no volume at which equating is both feasible and
valid — which would strengthen the case for
[[rec-mttc-two-year-escalation]] and the standard-setting route.
