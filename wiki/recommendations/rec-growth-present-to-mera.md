**The advice.** "Consider showing this to the Michigan Education Research Association (MERA)
group to get additional feedback."

**Why.** MERA is a Michigan research audience that would engage with the methodology on its
merits and is closer to the districts that consume growth results than a national technical
committee is. The committee is explicitly recommending a second, differently-situated review
before OEAA commits — appropriate given that the change would require an announced break in
trend that cannot be undone.

**Status.** Open.
