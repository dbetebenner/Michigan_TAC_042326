**The advice.** Find out how much the Smarter Balanced interim reports can be modified, and
whether the corresponding M-STEP score can be inserted into them.

**Why.** This is the highest-leverage item from Session 1. Every other concordance
recommendation improves a document that educators must find, read, and apply correctly. If the
interim report itself carried the concordant M-STEP score, the document would not be needed —
the number would appear where the teacher is already looking, at the moment they are looking.

**Blocker.** The answer belongs to Smarter Balanced, not to OEAA. The question was identified
in the room as one for the consortium and there is no record of it having been asked.

**Status.** Open. Owner: OEAA to raise with [[smarter-balanced]].
