**The advice.** Offered in answer to OEE's direct question, "What should be the overlap?" The
committee named a target band roughly **half** the ceiling in OEE's working definition, with the
qualification that it "has to be in association with the other variables; you might have to
adjust number of forms based upon percentage of overlap."

**Why.** Halving the ceiling is what makes "meaningfully different" mean anything — see
[[rec-mttc-define-defensible-inference]] for why the phrase currently has no criterion. The
qualification matters as much as the target: overlap is not independently settable. It trades
against how many forms a field can sustain, which trades against examinee volume and the
100-examinee equating threshold.

::: {.callout-note}
The specific target band, and the working-definition ceiling it halves, are test-security
sensitive and are held in the restricted layer — see `restricted/mttc-form-security-detail.md`.
:::

**Companion.** [[rec-mttc-fewer-forms-less-overlap]] states the direction of that trade.
