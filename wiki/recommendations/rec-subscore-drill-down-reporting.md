**The advice.** "Could provide a drill-down feature – basic information, subscore, standard
errors, learning profile."

**Why.** A structural answer to the tension the whole session circles: the field wants more
information, and more information reported flatly is misleading. Layering resolves it. The
default view carries what a general reader can act on; the detail — including the standard errors
that [[rec-subscore-report-certainty]] requires — is available to a reader who goes looking.

It also means the uncertainty information does not have to compete for space with the headline
result, which is usually why it gets cut.
