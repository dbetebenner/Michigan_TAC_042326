**The advice.** Automated item generation "would help generate a large number of questions as it
creates cousin items" — the committee's point being that if forms necessarily share most of
their items, attention should go to the minority that differ. (The remark names a specific
overlap figure; it is held verbatim in the restricted layer.)

**Why.** The overlap constraint is downstream of pool size. Automated item generation produces
families of cousin items from a common model, which is well suited to licensure content where
the construct is stable and the item shells are repeatable. It attacks the cause rather than the
symptom.

**The caveat the committee embedded.** Cousin items are cousins. Generating variants of the same
item model enlarges the pool without necessarily making forms *meaningfully* different — which
is why the recommendation is phrased around focusing on the non-overlapping 25%, and why it does
not by itself satisfy [[rec-mttc-redefine-per-incidence-group]].
