**The advice.** For districts already using the Smarter Balanced interim assessment, promote
the fact that they do not have to wait for reimbursement as they would when using other
assessments.

**Why.** A cash-flow advantage that costs OEAA nothing to communicate and that the target
audience — district financial staff, per
[[rec-interim-target-financial-staff-and-boards]] — is precisely the group that would value it.
The committee paired the two suggestions.

**Status.** Open.
