**The advice.** "This creates a lot of work, will be hard to do, and will be expensive; suggest
that this isn't necessary."

The committee advised **against** OEE's proposal to develop constructed-response item test forms
for the optional science and social studies disciplinary endorsements.

**Why.** Nine additional endorsement tests — biology, chemistry, Earth and space science,
physics, physical science, civics, economics, geography, history — each requiring item
development, rubric development, standard setting, and human scoring capacity. All of it for
fields whose examinee volumes are, by construction, smaller than the base tests that already
struggle to reach 100 examinees per form. See [[mttc-meaningfully-different-forms]].

OEE's own stated difficulty was the specificity CRIs require, and it came to the committee
asking for other options. The committee gave four:
[[rec-mttc-transcript-review-alternative]], [[rec-mttc-conjunctive-cut-scores]],
[[rec-mttc-random-standard-prompt]], [[rec-mttc-interview-guide-for-districts]].

**Status.** Declined — the committee's recommendation is against the proposal as put. The
underlying need is unresolved and depends on [[rec-mttc-define-endorsement-purpose]].
