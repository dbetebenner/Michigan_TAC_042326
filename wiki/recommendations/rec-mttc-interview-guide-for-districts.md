**The advice.** "Could develop a guide for districts for questions or information to ask during
interview."

**Why.** The most radical of the four alternatives, in that it declines to solve the problem
with an assessment at all. OEE's own account of what a passing MTTC score buys is that it "allows
a district to interview a candidate" — the hiring decision was always the district's. An
interview guide strengthens the decision at the point where it is actually made, without
building an instrument the state must then defend.

**Limit.** It does not produce an endorsement. If Michigan requires a credential on the
certificate, this supplements rather than substitutes — which is why it sits alongside
[[rec-mttc-conjunctive-cut-scores]] rather than replacing it.

**Status.** Open.
