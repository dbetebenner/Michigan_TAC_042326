**The advice.** Focus promotion on different organisational groups than have been targeted so
far — specifically district financial staff and school boards.

**Why.** Offered by OEE. Recruitment to date has been webinars, presentations, and direct
outreach, which reach assessment and curriculum staff. But the decision to adopt an interim
assessment programme is a budget decision made by people who do not attend assessment webinars.
Targeting the audience that actually holds the decision is a different strategy, not a louder
version of the current one.

**Status.** Open. MAC's barrier study may bear on which audience matters most.
