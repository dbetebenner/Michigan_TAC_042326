**The advice.** "It is better to have fewer forms with less overlap than many forms with lots of
overlap."

**Why.** This is the governing principle behind the committee's whole response to Session 4. The
contract requires OEE to *maximise* the number of meaningfully different forms, and OEE has been
reading that as maximising the count. The committee is saying the adjective binds harder than
the noun: three genuinely distinct forms serve security and fairness better than eight forms
that are variations of one.

It also resolves an apparent conflict in the group structure, where the highest-volume group is
permitted a form count carried over from the old contract rather than derived from Michigan's
own volumes.
