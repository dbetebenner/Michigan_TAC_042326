**The advice.** Districts and schools would want to see the formula used to produce the
concordance, not only the resulting lookup table.

**Why.** A lookup table is opaque. A district that cannot see how a Smarter Balanced score
becomes an M-STEP score cannot judge how much to trust any single cell, cannot explain the
mapping to a parent, and cannot detect when it is being applied outside its intended range.
Publishing the method converts the table from something to be believed into something that can
be checked.

**Status.** Open. No record of OEAA committing to publish the derivation.
