**The advice.** "Would like to see an example of score report for next meeting."

The final line of the April minutes, and one of two explicit commitments carried into the fall
meeting.

**Why.** Every reporting recommendation from Session 5 is a claim about how a document will be
read, and none of them can be evaluated in the abstract. A sample report makes
[[rec-subscore-report-certainty]], [[rec-subscore-drill-down-reporting]], and
[[rec-subscore-parent-think-alouds]] concrete at once — and it is also the artifact the parent
think-alouds would be run against.

**What to check it against.** Does a reported category carry its certainty? Is the At/Near
Standard band drawable for every subscore, and if not, is that visible? Does the layered
structure exist? Does the report state what it cannot support?

**Status.** Open — due at the fall 2026 meeting.
