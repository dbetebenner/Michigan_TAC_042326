**The advice.** "Set higher and conjunctive cut score (need at least 'X' on sub-areas using
current test)."

**Why.** Probably the strongest of the four alternatives. A candidate seeking a biology
endorsement must already pass the science test. Requiring a higher overall score *and* a minimum
on the relevant sub-area converts an existing instrument into an endorsement decision at
essentially zero development cost — no new items, no new rubrics, no scoring capacity.

**The obstacle nobody raised.** A conjunctive sub-area cut requires the sub-area score to be
reliable enough to bear a pass/fail decision for an individual. That is precisely the property
Session 5 spent a morning establishing that M-STEP claim-level subscores do *not* have — see
[[m-step-subscore-reporting]]. The MTTC is a different instrument with different sub-area
lengths, so the objection does not transfer automatically, but it does need answering before
this route is taken. The corpus contains no MTTC sub-area reliability evidence.

**Status.** Open.
