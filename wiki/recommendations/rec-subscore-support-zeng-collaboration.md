**The advice.** "JI Zengs's work is very sophisticated, and TAC would like to support her in her
work. Damion Betebenner suggested that she work with him on this."

(Both names are misspelled in the source; corrected here as Ji Zeng and Damian Betebenner. See
[[tac-meeting-notes-april-2026]].)

**Why.** This is the only place in the entire meeting record where an individual TAC member is
named taking an action, and the only recommendation addressed to the committee itself rather
than to a state office.

It is also the mechanism by which [[rec-subscore-model-joint-density]] gets done. That
recommendation asks for joint-density modelling with correctly derived marginals — a substantial
piece of methodological work that a single state psychometrician would carry alone. The
collaboration is what makes the technical advice actionable rather than aspirational.

**Status.** Accepted — offered and, on the record, welcomed. Whether the collaboration has begun
is not recorded and should be confirmed at the next meeting.
