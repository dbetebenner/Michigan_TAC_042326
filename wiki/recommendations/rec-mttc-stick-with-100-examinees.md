**The advice.** Asked whether Pearson should wait to reach 200 examinees per year for
high/medium incidence fields, or whether 100 is enough: "Stick with 100 because it will get you
more forms."

**Why.** 100 is the stated minimum for statistical equating. Waiting for 200 buys precision on
each form at the cost of halving the rate at which new forms enter service — and form scarcity,
not equating precision, is the problem the programme is trying to solve. Given
[[rec-mttc-fewer-forms-less-overlap]], the committee is not asking for many marginal forms; it
is asking that the forms which do exist not be delayed for a precision gain that does not
address the security risk.

**Status.** Open.
