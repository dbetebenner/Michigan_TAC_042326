**The advice.** "Establish scale, establish cuts, use theta estimate to categorize student
performance."

**Why.** An ordering discipline. Categories should be a consequence of a defined scale and
defined cut points on that scale, not a construct produced directly by a classification
procedure. Getting the order right means the cut points can be inspected, defended, and held
stable across years — and it means the reported category has a scale-score interpretation behind
it rather than being the raw output of a classifier.

Noted alongside: Smarter Balanced is using scaled score.
