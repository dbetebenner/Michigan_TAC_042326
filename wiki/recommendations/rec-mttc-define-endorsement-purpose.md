**The advice.** "OEE needs to define the purpose of the assessment": *What does having the
endorsement on your certificate signify?*

The answer offered in the room: it means that you know the content that is in the standards.

**Why.** That answer is the reason the committee rejected the constructed-response proposal. If
the endorsement certifies content knowledge against a set of standards, a selected-response
instrument with a conjunctive cut can support the claim — see
[[rec-mttc-conjunctive-cut-scores]]. Constructed response would be required only if the
endorsement certified something a candidate must *produce*: an argument, a lesson, an analysis.
It does not, on OEE's own account.

This is the endorsement-side twin of [[rec-mttc-define-defensible-inference]]. Both say the same
thing: name the claim before choosing the instrument.

**Status.** Open.
