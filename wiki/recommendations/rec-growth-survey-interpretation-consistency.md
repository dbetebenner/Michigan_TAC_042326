**The advice.** "Could you survey across schools to determine if elementary schools and high
schools interpreting their data the same way?"

**Why.** The same AGP means something structurally different at grade 4 and grade 11 — one has
three years of runway, one has one — and the corpus contains no evidence that schools
understand this. If high schools read their targets as a comparable statement of expectation
rather than as a compressed one, then the over-identification problem has a communication
component as well as a methodological one, and NGA would fix only half of it.
