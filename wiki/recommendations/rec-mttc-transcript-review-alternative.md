**The advice.** "You could review transcripts to verify that candidate actually took classes in
the specific area."

**Why.** The cheapest of the four alternatives. If the endorsement signifies content knowledge
in a discipline, evidence that the candidate completed coursework in that discipline is direct
evidence, already documented, requiring no new instrument.

**The weakness the committee named.** "There is an issue that higher education has the ability
to create their educator preparation program in whatever way they want; they are not aligned
across programs." A transcript line means different things at different institutions, so
transcript review substitutes an unstandardised measure for a standardised one.

That non-alignment is also, note, part of why the endorsement test exists at all — so this
alternative trades a known problem for the problem the test was built to solve.
