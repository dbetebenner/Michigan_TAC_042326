**The advice.** "Would rather see a system that supports all kids."

Offered directly against OEAA's account of the AGP's origin: it "was an attempt to communicate
the urgency to make improvements."

**Why.** A metric designed to communicate urgency and a metric designed to guide instruction
are different instruments, and Michigan has been using one for both. Urgency-signalling
tolerates targets that cannot be met — that is what makes them urgent. Instructional guidance
does not.

Paired with the committee's broader position: "Schools should not be focusing on proficiency;
their goal is to produce and maximize growth."

**Why it is hard.** The AGP is embedded in a federally approved accountability plan whose
purpose *is* identification. See [[essa-plan-constraints]]. Reorienting the metric toward
support without changing the plan means the same number carries two incompatible jobs.

**Status.** Open. This is a framing recommendation rather than an actionable one, and it is the
premise underneath most of the session's other growth advice.
