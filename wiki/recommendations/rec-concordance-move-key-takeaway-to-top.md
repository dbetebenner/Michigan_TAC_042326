**The advice.** Move the key takeaway and the performance level information to the top of the
document.

**Why.** Both draft layouts lead with explanatory narrative and place the actionable content
below it. The audience is educators reading between other tasks; the number they need and what
it means should be the first thing on the page, with the caveats following. This is the
cheapest of all the session's recommendations to implement.

**Status.** Open. Two layouts were under consideration
([[interim-concordance-table-examples]]); neither leads with the takeaway.
