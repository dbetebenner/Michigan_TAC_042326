**The advice.** "Encourage OEAA to approach this avenue."

The committee's endorsement of the [[nearest-grade-average-method]] as the path forward for
high school growth targets.

**Why.** NGA lets every high school grade project three years out, matching elementary and
middle school, which addresses the structural cause of high schools being over-identified. It
is more precise than the logistic regression method already in operational use for MI-Access,
and it has a Michigan precedent — the 2018-19 PSAT 8/9 and SAT transition. The alignment
scatterplots showed the NGA and R-package AGPs tracking closely.

**Status.** Accepted in principle by the committee. Not yet adopted by OEAA, and adoption is
gated by [[essa-plan-constraints]] and by an unanswered question about whether the resulting
impact on index values is desirable ([[q-growth-desirable-impact]]).

**Carries with it.** The committee's endorsement of the method was paired with
[[rec-growth-adequate-target-above-65]] — the method should not be adopted while leaving the
adequacy threshold where it is.
