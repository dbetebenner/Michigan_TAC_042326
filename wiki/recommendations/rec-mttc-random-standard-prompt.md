**The advice.** "Develop an assessment where Pearson/OEE randomly select a standard and use this
as a prompt for open-ended questions."

**Why.** The most developed of the four alternatives, and the one that preserves a
constructed-response format at a fraction of the cost. Rather than building nine bespoke CRI
tests, one mechanism generates prompts from the standards themselves.

The committee worked through the detail in the room:

- Social studies standards "aren't all written as questions," so the task would evaluate the
  skill of developing an argument rather than recall.
- That skill "could apply to any of the four fields" — one instrument serves civics, economics,
  geography, and history.
- Scoring is the obstacle: "There would be an issue with finding someone able to score these,
  but a rubric could be developed."
- [[pearson]] "already has rubrics developed for other CRI items."
- Asked how the cut would be established: from the rubric.

**A variant not pursued.** "Could they be performance items vs. CRI (example: develop a lesson
plan)?" — raised and not taken up.
