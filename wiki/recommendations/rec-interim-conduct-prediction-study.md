**The advice.** Consider completing a prediction study, if there are enough participants to
support one.

**Why.** Raised in response to the observation that other systems make predictions of student
performance on the summative test. A prediction study would replace the concordance table's
"could score" with a quantified relationship, which would answer the committee's question about
what *could* means and would let OEAA state the imperfection the table currently only gestures
at.

**Caveat carried with it.** OEAA's stated preference — and the committee's — is for the
present-tense framing: what a student would score *today*, rather than at the end of the year.
A prediction study should not be allowed to convert the interim into a forecasting instrument
at the expense of its instructional use.

**Constraint.** Conditional on participation volume. With roughly 14,000 students across 21
schools and flat year-over-year participation, sample adequacy is not obvious.

**Status.** Open.
