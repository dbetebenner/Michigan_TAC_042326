**State as of April 2026:** stable operations, an unresolved budget, an unfunded reporting
mandate, and a new superintendent whose priorities are not yet known.

Standing thread for the department-level context reported in [[2026-04-mde-update]].

## Spring 2026 testing

Status quo, with one exception: the new WIN Work Readiness Assessments. WIN will attend the
fall TAC meeting and is expected to bring its psychometric team —
[[q-win-fall-attendance]].

## Budget

In process at the time of the meeting. Asked whether MDE's budget would be cut, the answer was
that this is an annual occurrence, that the process is early, and that nothing is final.

## Benchmark assessments — the unfunded mandate

MDE still reimburses districts for benchmark assessment use. Schools are **currently required
to report the data**, but that requirement is **not in this year's budget**.

A reporting obligation without an appropriation behind it is a standing compliance risk: data
quality degrades when the reporting is unfunded, and the resulting dataset carries a
selection problem that is easy to forget by the time anyone analyses it.

## Leadership

Michigan is in the initial period of new State Superintendent Dr. Glenn Malayko, who is
focused on gathering feedback from districts. OEAA has had an initial conversation with him
about reporting.

That conversation is more consequential than its single line suggests. Three of the five
sessions at this meeting turn on reporting decisions:
[[m-step-subscore-reporting]] has a spring decision deadline;
[[m-step-smarter-concordance]] is a reporting artifact under active revision;
[[adequate-growth-targets]] would require an announced break in trend and a change to what
schools see. A new superintendent's reporting priorities could reopen any of them.
