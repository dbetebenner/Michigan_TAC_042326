**State as of April 2026:** flat participation, an active barrier study, and a committee view
that OEAA is solving the wrong problem.

## Where adoption stands

Roughly 14,000 students across 21 schools in about 10 districts. Rebranded from a "pilot" to a
professional learning opportunity delivered with the [[michigan-assessment-consortium]] —
a deliberate move away from language implying provisional status. Recruitment has been
webinars, presentations, and direct outreach, with incentives offered. The pitch explicitly
does not ask districts to replace what they already use.

Named proponents: Forest Hills and North Branch Area Schools.

## The number that is not in the presentation

[[smarter-balanced-interim-participation-data]] shows total tests moving from 14,756 to
14,495 — down about 2% — in a year when Forest Hills brought six entirely new schools into the
programme. Ruth Fox Middle School fell 1,418 tests, about half its volume; Whiteford Middle
School more than doubled.

New districts joining are being cancelled out by existing districts contracting. That is a
retention problem. The recruitment strategy on the slide addresses awareness.

## What OEAA cannot see

Participation data exists at the school level but **not at the user level**. Asked how many
educators are actually participating, the answer was that the data is not available. So OEAA
cannot distinguish broad shallow use from narrow deep use, and cannot tell whether the Ruth
Fox decline is a lost district or a changed practice within one.

Tracked as [[q-interim-user-level-participation]].

## The barrier study

MAC is running a study to determine what prevents districts and schools from using the
assessment. It is the correct instrument and it was already underway at the time of the
meeting; no results are in the corpus.

## What the committee suggested

The committee's adoption advice pointed at money and governance rather than at teachers.

- [[rec-interim-target-financial-staff-and-boards]] — target district financial staff and school boards
- [[rec-interim-promote-no-reimbursement-wait]] — districts need not wait for reimbursement, unlike other assessments
- [[rec-interim-conduct-prediction-study]] — run a prediction study if participation supports it

OEAA floated a stronger version: universal use, with per-student money attached for
instructional support and resources. It drew no recorded committee response.

## The connection to reporting

The committee's read is that adoption follows usefulness, and usefulness depends on what the
score report tells a teacher. That is why the session's centre of gravity moved to
[[m-step-smarter-concordance]] — and why the most consequential suggestion was to ask Smarter
Balanced whether the interim reports could carry the corresponding M-STEP score directly
([[rec-interim-ask-sbac-embed-mstep-score]]), which would make the concordance table
unnecessary.

Interim assessments are tied to Tools for Teachers, the instructional resource educators
access to support instruction, which is the intended path from score to action.
