Vendor for the Michigan Test for Teacher Certification under contract to [[oee]]. Represented at
[[2026-04-23-tac-meeting]] by Nancy Hahn and Heather Klesch of Evaluation Systems, and Jackie
Thompson of Pearson Professional Assessments.

The contract requires OEE to maximise the number of meaningfully different test forms per field —
the requirement that generated [[mttc-meaningfully-different-forms]].

Pearson asked two questions of the committee that remain open: what definition and item overlap
an equating methodology would require, and whether equating data can become too old
([[q-mttc-data-staleness]]).

Pearson conducts the standard settings for Groups 3 and 4 and already holds rubrics for
constructed-response items, which bears on
[[rec-mttc-random-standard-prompt]].
