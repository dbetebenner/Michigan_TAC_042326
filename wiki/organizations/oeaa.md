The MDE office responsible for Michigan's statewide assessment and accountability systems.
Director: [[andrew-middlestead]].

Owns four of the five sessions at [[2026-04-23-tac-meeting]] and is the addressee of most of the
committee's recommendations. Its accountability unit runs the growth model
([[michigan-growth-model]]); its psychometric staff run the subscore work
([[m-step-subscore-reporting]]); its assessment staff run interim adoption
([[smarter-balanced-interim-adoption]]).

OEAA is the client for this TAC. The purpose of the three-times-yearly meeting is for OEAA staff
and its vendors to bring issues to the committee and receive advice.
