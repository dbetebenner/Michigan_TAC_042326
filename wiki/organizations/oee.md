The MDE office responsible for educator preparation and certification, including the Michigan
Test for Teacher Certification. Represented at [[2026-04-23-tac-meeting]] by
[[bridgett-mcdowell]], its only attendee.

OEE holds the MTTC contract with [[pearson]] and brought both MTTC questions to the April
meeting: [[mttc-meaningfully-different-forms]] and
[[mttc-constructed-response-endorsements]].

The committee's central advice to OEE was to define the inference the MTTC defends before
settling any question about forms or endorsements —
[[rec-mttc-define-defensible-inference]].
