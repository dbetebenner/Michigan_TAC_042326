Michigan research association. The committee recommended presenting the growth methodology work
to MERA for additional feedback — [[rec-growth-present-to-mera]].

The rationale is that MERA is closer to the districts that consume growth results than a national
technical committee is, and would provide a second, differently situated review before OEAA
commits to a change that would require an announced break in trend.
