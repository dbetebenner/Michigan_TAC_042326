The consortium behind the ACCESS English language proficiency assessment and its associated
screeners, used in Michigan for English learner identification and progress measurement.

Two operational items were reported at [[2026-04-23-tac-meeting]] — the release of the WIDA
Alternate Screener for students with significant cognitive disabilities, and a second year of the
extended 10-week ACCESS testing window. See [[wida-assessment-operations]].

WIDA ACCESS also carries growth targets in Michigan, computed by quantile regression through the
R SGP package ([[michigan-growth-model]]), and is covered by the
[[nearest-grade-average-method]] proposal in grades 6–8 for alignment checks and grades 10–12 for
impact evaluation.
