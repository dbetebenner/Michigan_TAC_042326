Multi-state assessment consortium. Michigan uses its interim assessments as a voluntary offering
to districts ([[smarter-balanced-interim-adoption]]) and its subscore performance indicator
method as the basis of current M-STEP claim and domain reporting
([[m-step-subscore-reporting]]).

Two open items involve the consortium directly:

- Whether the interim reports can be modified to carry the corresponding M-STEP score —
  [[rec-interim-ask-sbac-embed-mstep-score]]. Identified in the room as "a question for Smarter
  Balanced" and not yet asked.
- The SBAC subscore indicator method was outperformed by CT-PO on both deviance against real
  data and classification accuracy and consistency across every simulated condition —
  [[ct-po-vs-sbac-simulation-deck]].

Smarter Balanced interim assessments are tied to Tools for Teachers, the instructional resource
that gives the interim score a path to action.
