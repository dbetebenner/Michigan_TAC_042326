The state education agency. Two of its offices convene this Technical Advisory Committee jointly:
the Office of Educational Assessment and Accountability ([[oeaa]]) and the Office of Educator
Excellence ([[oee]]).

Led at the time of [[2026-04-23-tac-meeting]] by new State Superintendent Dr. Glenn Malayko,
still in his initial period and focused on gathering district feedback. OEAA had held an initial
conversation with him about reporting.
