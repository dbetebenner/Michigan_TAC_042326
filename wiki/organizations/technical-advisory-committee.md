The joint Technical Advisory Committee for MDE's Office of Educational Assessment and
Accountability ([[oeaa]]) and Office of Educator Excellence ([[oee]]).

**Purpose.** The committee meets three times a year. Vendors and state staff present issues to
the committee and receive feedback and advice on what is being done or proposed. It is advisory:
it does not decide, and the April record contains no votes or motions.

**Members recorded in April 2026.** [[mark-reckase]], [[jennifer-randall]], [[gregory-cizek]],
[[damian-betebenner]], [[martha-thurlow]].

**How the committee appears in the record.** As a body. The minutes attribute every question and
every recommendation to "TAC," never to an individual, with one exception — see
[[rec-subscore-support-zeng-collaboration]]. This is a property of the minuting convention rather
than of the meeting, and it means the corpus can record *what* the committee advised but not
which expertise produced the advice. That limitation is the starting point for the argument in
`documents/ai-native-tac.qmd`.
