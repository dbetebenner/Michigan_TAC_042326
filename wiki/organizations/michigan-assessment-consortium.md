Michigan organisation partnering with [[oeaa]] on the Smarter Balanced interim assessment
programme.

The partnership is what allowed the rebranding of the programme from a "pilot" to a professional
learning opportunity — a deliberate move away from language implying provisional status. See
[[smarter-balanced-interim-adoption]].

MAC is also conducting the **barrier study** to determine what prevents districts and schools
from using the interim assessment. It was underway at the time of
[[2026-04-smarter-balanced-interim]]; no results appear in this corpus. Given that participation
is flat and OEAA cannot see usage at the user level
([[q-interim-user-level-participation]]), the barrier study is the main instrument Michigan has
for understanding its own adoption problem.
