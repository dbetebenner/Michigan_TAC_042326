## The through-line

Three of April's five sessions asked the same question in different vocabularies: *when a
measurement system is asked to carry more weight than its precision can bear, what should the
state do?* High school growth targets, licensure form security, and subscore reporting are all
instances. The committee's answers rhymed — define the inference you are defending, then choose
the instrument.

## A note on what is missing

The corpus can tell you what the committee advised. It cannot tell you which member advised it,
because the source minutes attribute to bodies rather than people. Five members with distinct
areas of expertise were in the room and the record flattens them into one voice. That gap is not
a defect of this wiki — it is the defect this wiki was built to demonstrate. See
`documents/ai-native-tac.qmd`.
