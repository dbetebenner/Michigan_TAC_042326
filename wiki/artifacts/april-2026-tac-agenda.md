## Where it disagrees with the minutes

The agenda is the better record of **what was scheduled**; the minutes are the better record
of **what was discussed**. They differ in four ways:

1. **Order.** The agenda runs growth at 9:30 and the crosswalk at 10:30. The minutes make
   the crosswalk Session 1 and growth Session 3.
2. **Naming.** The agenda calls the Jennifer Paul session "M-STEP/Smarter Crosswalk." The
   deck and the minutes call it "Using the Smarter Balanced Interim Assessments," with the
   concordance table as one component.
3. **Coverage.** A [[wida-assessment-operations]] update appears in the minutes under
   Session 1 and is on no agenda item.
4. **Minute approval.** The agenda's 9:00am "Minute Approval" item is not recorded in the
   minutes at all.
