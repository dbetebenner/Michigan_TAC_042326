"Update on Michigan Student Growth Metrics." Created by Chad Bailey, last modified by
Kelley Holcomb (OEAA), on the MDE Logo and Blue Bars template. 26 slides, **eight of them
hidden**, three carrying speaker notes. Title slide dated 4/24/2026, though the session ran
on 4/23. Presented in [[2026-04-accountability-growth-targets]].

## Stated problem and purpose

> Michigan's current growth model results in far more aggressive growth targets for high
> school students. Introduce possible modifications to make high school growth targets more
> equitable. Discuss current vs possible modifications.

## Structure

Problem and purpose (2–4) → hidden refresher on the current model (5–12) → DRC explorations
(13–16) → MDE Nearest Grade Average (17–20) → four scatterplot slides (21–24) → NGA
application summary (25) → discussion questions (26).

## The eight hidden slides

Slides 5–12 were skipped live. They are the canonical statement of Michigan's current growth
model and are the best available background for [[michigan-growth-model]] — state-level uses,
metric definitions, the two current target methods with their build steps, the method/assessment
mapping, the precision comparison, and the timeframe rationale.

## Content found only in speaker notes

Neither of these appears in the minutes or on any visible slide.

**On slide 6, concerning state-level uses of growth:**

> Educator Evaluations were repealed from state law. Districts may now conduct those however
> based solely on local policy and do not report them to the state. School grades were also
> repealed from state law.

This changes the stakes picture materially. Two of the uses that historically justified the
growth model's precision requirements no longer exist in statute.

**On slide 20, concerning the WIDA application timeline:**

> By 2026-27 WIDA NGA AGPs would be applied to grades 8-12. However, we are choosing to limit
> to data post covid (i.e., 2021-22 and forward). Meaning as of 2024-25, the most we can
> project out is 3 years.

This is the operational reason the English Learner impact is expected to grow — the deck's
summary slide asserts the growth without giving the mechanism, and the mechanism is here.

## The four scatterplots

Slides 21–24 are images with no extractable text: content-area model alignment, content-area
model application, English Learner model alignment, English Learner model application, each
a Growth Index scatter comparing R SGP package AGPs against Nearest Grade Average AGPs. The
minutes characterise them as showing "precision between the two is pretty close" for
alignment and "meaningful impact on overall rating" for application.

## Discussion questions posed

> What concerns do you have with these methods?
> Are there ways we could mitigate those concerns for these methods?
> Ideas for other methods to extend years projected for high school grades? (Balancing
> complexity and the need for transparency and accessibility)

## A contradiction with the minutes

Slide 14 lists predictors `SGP 2024 and SGP 2025` and outcome `SGP 2025`. That is
self-contradictory. The minutes' `SGP 2023 and SGP 2024` predicting `SGP 2025` is coherent
and is what this corpus follows. See [[tac-meeting-notes-april-2026]].
