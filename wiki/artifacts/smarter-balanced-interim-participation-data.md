Session handout for [[2026-04-smarter-balanced-interim]]. A single table: District, School,
Tests Completed 24-25, Tests Completed 25-26, Difference, Percent Change. Twenty-one school
rows across ten districts — matching the "~14,000 students across 21 schools" and
"approximately 10 districts" in the record.

## Districts represented

Akron-Fairgrove, Big Jackson, Corunna, Forest Hills (six schools, all new this year),
Hartland Consolidated (six schools), Lawton, North Branch Area (two), Stanton Township,
Whiteford Agricultural (two).

## The headline the table does not print

Totals move from 14,756 tests to 14,495 — a net change of −261, about −2%. Participation is
**flat**, and it is flat in a year when Forest Hills brought six entirely new schools into
the programme. Growth from new districts is being cancelled by contraction in existing ones.

Two movements drive it:

- Ruth Fox Middle School: −1,418 tests, −50%
- Whiteford Middle School: +264 tests, +128%

Round Elementary also fell about 26%.

That pattern — a large existing user halving its usage while new districts join — is a
retention problem, not an awareness problem, and it bears directly on the barrier study
discussed in [[smarter-balanced-interim-adoption]].

## Footnote in the source

> *NA = Did not participate in 24-25   0* = Unsure if participating for 25-26

Two rows carry the "unsure" marker, so the 25-26 figure is provisional.
