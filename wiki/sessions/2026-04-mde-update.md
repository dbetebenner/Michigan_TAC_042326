**Presenter:** Andrew Middlestead (OEAA) · **Day:** April 23, 2026

The only session with no accompanying artifact. Scheduled on the agenda for 11:15am.

## Spring 2026 testing status

Status quo, with one exception: the new WIN Work Readiness Assessments. WIN will attend the
fall TAC meeting, and — at the committee's expectation — will bring its psychometric team.
That commitment is tracked as [[q-win-fall-attendance]].

## State budget

In process. Asked directly whether the MDE budget would be cut, Middlestead's answer was that
this happens every year, that the process is early, and that nothing is final — which is
typical.

## Benchmark assessments

MDE is still reimbursing districts for benchmark assessment use. Schools are currently
required to report the data, but the requirement is **not funded in this year's budget**.
A reporting mandate without an appropriation is a durable source of compliance risk and is
tracked as a standing thread in [[mde-assessment-operations]].

## Department leadership

Michigan is still in the initial period of new State Superintendent Dr. Glenn Malayko, who is
focused on gathering feedback from districts. OEAA has had an initial conversation with him
about reporting.

That conversation matters more than its one line in the minutes suggests. Three other sessions
at this meeting — growth targets, subscore reporting, the concordance table — turn on
reporting decisions that a new superintendent's priorities could reopen.

## What the committee advised

Nothing formally. This session is briefing, not consultation.
