## What OEAA was actually asking

The deck reduces to one question:

> What information would educators need in order to better use the scores from the interims?

Behind it sits a harder one OEAA did not put on a slide: adoption is flat, and OEAA does not
know why. Usage data exists at the school level but not at the user level, so the department
cannot see whether 14,000 tests represent many teachers using the system lightly or a few
using it heavily.

## The substance

Adoption was rebranded from "pilot" to a professional learning opportunity run with the
[[michigan-assessment-consortium]] — a deliberate move away from framing that implied
provisional status. Roughly 14,000 students across 21 schools and about 10 districts.
Recruitment has been webinars, presentations, and direct outreach, with incentives offered.
Forest Hills and North Branch Area Schools are named as proponents.

The participation table tells a story the presentation did not: net tests fell about 2% even
though Forest Hills brought six new schools in. See
[[smarter-balanced-interim-participation-data]].

MAC is running a barrier study to determine what prevents districts from using the assessment.

Full thread: [[smarter-balanced-interim-adoption]].

## The concordance table

OEAA's instrument for raising adoption is a table mapping Smarter Balanced interim scale
scores to M-STEP scale scores by grade and performance level. The committee treated it as a
communication artifact rather than a psychometric one, and its feedback was almost entirely
about how a non-specialist would read it.

Full thread: [[m-step-smarter-concordance]].

## What the committee advised

- [[rec-concordance-publish-formula]] — districts will want the formula, not just the table
- [[rec-concordance-move-key-takeaway-to-top]] — performance-level information belongs at the top
- [[rec-concordance-build-theta-tool]] — build a tool that accepts a theta value
- [[rec-interim-ask-sbac-embed-mstep-score]] — ask Smarter Balanced whether the interim reports themselves can carry the corresponding M-STEP score
- [[rec-interim-conduct-prediction-study]] — run a prediction study if participation supports it
- [[rec-interim-target-financial-staff-and-boards]] — target district financial staff and school boards
- [[rec-interim-promote-no-reimbursement-wait]] — tell districts they need not wait for reimbursement

The committee also pressed on the word *could* in the table's worked example, and on what
"most likely score" is supposed to mean to a teacher. The exchange is short in the minutes
and is the clearest instance in the meeting of a validity question arriving disguised as a
copy-editing question.
