Two days at The Graduate, 133 Evergreen Ave., East Lansing, Michigan — Lemon Tree Room.
Five substantive sessions, four of them on day one.

## What the meeting was about

Three of the five sessions were, underneath their different subject matter, the same
question: **when a measurement system is asked to carry more weight than its precision can
bear, what should the state do?**

- [[2026-04-accountability-growth-targets]] — high school growth targets are more aggressive
  than elementary ones because there is less time to remediate, and the accountability index
  is consequently identifying high schools at a rate OEAA does not believe reflects reality.
- [[2026-04-mttc-meaningfully-different-forms]] — a licensure program must produce
  "meaningfully different" test forms for fields that do not generate enough examinees to
  equate them.
- [[2026-04-subtest-performance-indicators]] — the field wants subscores from a test
  deliberately engineered to be unidimensional.

The committee's answers rhymed across all three: be honest about what the numbers can
support, define the inference you are actually defending, and prefer less information
accurately reported over more information reported badly.

The other two sessions were adoption and operations —
[[2026-04-smarter-balanced-interim]] and [[2026-04-mde-update]].
